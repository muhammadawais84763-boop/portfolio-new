Workflow

A smooth, collaborative process from idea to final delivery.

01

Project Discussion

We define the purpose of the video, the people it needs to reach, where it will be published, and the action or feeling it should leave with viewers.

02

Footage Review

I review the supplied footage, audio, brand materials, and references to understand what is usable, what is missing, and how the story can be structured.

03

Editing and Storytelling

The first cut selects and arranges the strongest moments, removes distractions, and establishes a pace appropriate for the intended audience and runtime.

04

Sound Design and Color Grading

After the structure is approved, color and audio are refined for a consistent, clear finish. Titles, captions, and motion graphics are included only as agreed in the scope.

05

Client Review

You review the draft against the brief and send one consolidated set of notes per included revision round. Changes outside the agreed direction are discussed before extra work begins.

06

Final Delivery

Final files are checked and exported in the agreed aspect ratios, resolution, and formats. Extra cutdowns, source project files, and alternate versions are provided only when listed in the quote.
