How Pricing Works

Your quote is based on the work, not a surprise add-on.

SCOPE

What you need

Video length, number of versions, aspect ratios, captions, graphics, and complexity affect the time and deliverables.

MATERIAL

What you provide

Footage volume and condition, music or asset licensing requirements, brand guidelines, and how organized the source files are all inform the estimate.

SCHEDULE

When you need it

Agree on a realistic schedule and feedback windows. Rush availability, if possible, is confirmed explicitly in the quote.

These dollar figures are unconfirmed example starting estimates, not guaranteed rates or a binding price list. They are not tied to a guaranteed runtime, footage quantity, turnaround, or specific editing workload until those details are agreed. Ask for a written quote before booking.

A written quote should identify the video count and approximate lengths, supplied-material assumptions, edits and cutdowns, aspect ratios and export formats, captions and graphics, schedule, feedback rounds, payment terms, and any licensed music or stock costs. Work outside that scope is discussed and approved before it is added. Taxes, transfer fees, or third-party licensing should also be clarified where applicable.

Pricing Questions

Useful details before you book.

What counts as a revision?

A revision is a consolidated list of adjustments to the agreed edit after a review cut. A new creative direction, replacement footage, or additional version may require extra time and a revised quote. The written quote should say how many rounds are included.

Can I request a custom package?

Yes. Share the number of videos, approximate final lengths, platforms and aspect ratios, footage status, graphics or caption needs, and target date. The deliverables can then be scoped to match the project.

Are music, stock footage, and paid assets included?

Do not assume they are included. The quote should state whether music, stock footage, fonts, voiceover, or other licensed assets are needed, who obtains the license, and who pays any third-party fee.

How long will editing take?

Timing depends on the length and condition of the footage, the agreed work, and how quickly feedback is provided. A delivery date is confirmed in the quote rather than promised by the package name.

How do I secure a booking?

Review and accept the written project scope and payment terms. The start date, payment milestones, and file-transfer method should be confirmed before editing begins.
