Included Only When Agreed

Know what is part of your edit before the project begins.

A service category is a starting point, not an unlimited bundle. Your written quote defines exactly what is included.

SOURCE FILES

Footage and assets

You provide the footage and brand materials you have permission to use. Stock media, music licenses, voiceover recording, filming, and third-party purchases are discussed separately.

REVISIONS

Feedback and changes

The quote states how many consolidated feedback rounds are included. New footage, additional formats, or a change to an already approved direction may require more time and a revised estimate.

DELIVERY

Exports and project files

Resolution, aspect ratio, file type, versions, and deadline are agreed up front. Editable project files and extra cutdowns are not automatic deliverables unless listed.
