What happens after you get in touch?

01 · Reply

Review the brief

We clarify goals, deliverables, source files, references, intended audience, and any open questions. If information is missing, we identify it before estimating.

02 · Quote

Confirm the scope

You review a written plan describing each final file, aspect ratio, included graphics or captions, feedback rounds, expected timing, price, and any licensing costs.

03 · Create

Start the edit

After the scope and booking terms are accepted and the agreed assets are available, work moves through draft, consolidated feedback, final approval, and export.
